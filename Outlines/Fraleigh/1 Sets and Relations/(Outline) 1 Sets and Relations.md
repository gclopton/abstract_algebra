

- [[The Notion of a Set]]
- It is not feasible (here) to push the definition of everything back to the concept of a set (e.g. defining $\pi$ in terms of a set)
- Every definition is an _if and only if_ statement.
	- they are often stated with the _only if_ suppressed
	- example "A triangle is isosceles if it has two sides of equal length"
