

Theorem 52: Let $X \rightarrow \operatorname{Spec}(A)$. Then there are bijections

$$
\begin{aligned}
& \operatorname{Hom}_{\operatorname{Sch}_A}\left(X, \mathbb{P}_A^n\right) \cong\left\{\text { equiv. classes of surjections }\left[\mathcal{O}_X^{\oplus(n+1)} \rightarrow \mathcal{L}\right] \mid \mathcal{L} \text { invertible sheaf }\right\} \\
\cong & \left\{\text { iso classes of tuples }\left(\mathcal{L}, s_0, \ldots, s_n\right) \mid \mathcal{L} \text { invertible sheaf, } s_i \text { are global sections which generate } \mathcal{L}\right\} .
\end{aligned}
$$

proof. The sets on RHS are clearly bijective.
Write $\mathbb{P}_A^n=\operatorname{Proj}\left(A\left[z_0, \ldots, z_n\right]\right)$ and note that the sections
$x_0, \ldots, x_n \in \Gamma\left(\mathbb{P}^n, \mathcal{O}(1)\right)$ generate $\mathcal{O}(1)=A\left\langle x_0, \ldots, x_n\right\rangle$ (so $\mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^n}^{\oplus^{(n+1)}} \rightarrow \mathcal{O}(1)$ is surjective).

Now given $f: X \rightarrow \mathbb{P}_A^n$ we get $\mathcal{O}_X^{\oplus(n+1)} \rightarrow f^* \mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^n}(1) \cong \mathcal{L}$.
On other hand, given a surjection $\mathcal{O}_X^{\oplus(n+1)} \rightarrow \mathcal{L}$ corresponding to sections $s_0, \ldots, s_n$, note that since the $s_i$ generate $\mathcal{L}$, we have that $X=\bigcup X_{s_i}$.

Writing

$$
\left.D_{+}\left(x_i\right) \cong \operatorname{Spec} A\left[y_0, \ldots, \hat{y}_i, \ldots, y_n\right] \quad \text { (i.e. } y_j=x_j / x_i\right)
$$

we define $X_{s_i} \rightarrow U_i$ by

$$
\Gamma\left(X_{s_i}, \mathcal{O}_X\right) \longleftarrow A\left[y_0, \ldots, \hat{y}_i, \ldots, y_n\right], \quad y_j \longmapsto s_j / s_i .
$$

note: $s_i$ determines the map $s_i: \mathcal{O}_X \rightarrow \mathcal{L}$, and since $s_i \notin \mathfrak{m}_x \mathcal{L}_x$ and $\mathcal{L}$ is locally free of rank one, we have

$$
s_i:\left.\left.\mathcal{O}_X\right|_{X_{s_i}} \xrightarrow{\cong} \mathcal{L}\right|_{X_{s_i}}
$$

(since $\left(s_i\right)_x$ is a basis, $\mathcal{O}_{X, x} \cdot s_i \cong \mathcal{L}_x$ for $x \in X_{s_i}$ ).



---


These maps patch together to yield $X \rightarrow \mathbb{P}_A^n$. This map only depends on $\left(\mathcal{L}, s_0, \ldots, s_n\right)$ up to iso and these are inverse assignments.

Proposition 53
Let $\psi: X \rightarrow \mathbb{P}_A^n=\operatorname{Proj}\left(A\left[z_0, \ldots, z_n\right]\right)$ be the map determined by $\left(\mathcal{L}, s_0, \ldots, s_n\right)$. Then $\psi$ is an immersion if there is a subset $J \subset\{0,1, \ldots, n\}$ such that:
(i) $X_{s_j}$ is affine for all $j \in J$ and $X=\bigcup_{j \in J} X_{s_j}$, and
(ii)

$$
\begin{aligned}
A\left[y_0, \ldots, \widehat{y_j}, \ldots, y_n\right] \longrightarrow \Gamma\left(X_{s_j}, \mathcal{O}_X\right) & \cong \Gamma\left(X_{s_j}, \mathcal{L}\right) \\
y_i & \longmapsto \frac{s_i}{s_j}
\end{aligned}
$$

is surjective.
Moreover, $\psi$ is a closed immersion $\Longleftrightarrow J$ can be chosen to be $\{0,1, \ldots, n\}$.
Proof: For $j \in J$, these conditions $\Rightarrow X_{s_j}$ closed subscheme of $D_{+}\left(x_j\right)$

$$
\Rightarrow X=\bigcup_{j \in J} X_{s_j} \subseteq \bigcup_{j \in J} D_{+}\left(x_j\right)=: U \subseteq \mathbb{P}_A^n
$$

(closed subschem, $U$ open).
If $J=\{0, \ldots, n\}$ then $U=\mathbb{P}_A^n \Rightarrow \psi$ closed immersion.
On other hand, if $\psi$ is closed then $X_{s_i}=X \cap D_{+}\left(x_i\right) \subseteq D_{+}\left(x_i\right) \cong \mathbb{A}^n$ (closed)
$\Rightarrow X_{s_i}$ is affine and the map in (2) is surjective.
□

Ample Line Bundles
Def: An invertible sheaf $\mathcal{L}$ on a noetherian scheme $X$ is ample if for every coherent $\mathcal{F}, \exists N>0$ s.t. for all $n>N$,

$$
\mathcal{F} \otimes \mathcal{L}^{\otimes n}
$$

is generated by global sections.
Rem: If $X$ is qcqs define "ample" line bundle similarly but ask that any $\mathcal{F} \in \mathrm{Q} \operatorname{coh} X$, then $\mathcal{F} \otimes \mathcal{L}^{\otimes n}$ is generated by global secs for $n \gg N$.

Recall, Serre's thm: $X$ projective, $\mathcal{L}=\mathcal{O}_X(1)$ is a very ample.
Then $\mathcal{F} \otimes \mathcal{O}_X(1)^{\otimes n}$ is generated by global secs for all $n \gg 0$.
i.e. on a projective scheme, very ample ⇒ ample.
Proposition 54: Let $\mathcal{L}$ be an ample invertible sheaf on a noeth scheme $X$.
TFAE
(i) $\mathcal{L}$ is ample
(ii) $\mathcal{L}^{\otimes m}$ is ample $\forall m>0$
(iii) $\mathcal{L}^{\otimes m}$ is ample for some $m>0$.
proof: $(i) \Rightarrow(i i) \Rightarrow(i i i) \checkmark$
$(i i i) \Rightarrow(i)$ Consider $\mathcal{F} \otimes \mathcal{L}^{\otimes i}$. Since $\mathcal{L}^{\otimes m}$ ample, $\exists N_i>0$ s.t.

$$
\mathcal{F} \otimes \mathcal{L}^{\otimes i} \otimes \mathcal{L}^{\otimes m n}
$$

is generated by global secs $\forall n \geq N_i$.



---


Let $N=\max \left\{m N_0, \ldots, m N_{m-1}\right\}$. Then for any $k \geq N$

$$
\mathcal{F} \otimes \mathcal{L}^{\otimes k}
$$

is generated by global sects.

Prop 55 Let $\mathcal{L}$ be an invertible sheaf on a noetherian scheme $X$. TFAE
(i) $\mathcal{L}$ is ample
(ii) The open subsets $X_f \subset X$ for $f \in \Gamma\left(X, \mathcal{L}^{\otimes n}\right)$ and $n>0$ form a basis for topology on $X$.
(iii) $\exists d \geq 1$, finitely many $f_i \in \Gamma\left(X, \mathcal{L}^{\otimes d}\right)$ s.t. $X_{f_i}$ is affine and $X=\bigcup_i X_{f_i}$.
proof. $(i) \Rightarrow(i i)$
Let $x \in X, U$ open nbd of $x$ and $Z \subset X$ closed subscheme s.t. $X \backslash Z=U$.
Let $\mathcal{I}$ be ideal sheaf corresponding to $Z$. Then

$$
\Gamma\left(X, \mathcal{I} \otimes \mathcal{L}^{\otimes n}\right) \subset \Gamma\left(X, \mathcal{L}^{\otimes n}\right)
$$

and $\mathcal{I} \otimes \mathcal{L}^{\otimes n}$ is generated by global sections.
Since $\mathcal{L}$ is invertible, tensoring with $\mathcal{L}^{\otimes n}$ gives

$$
\begin{aligned}
& 0 \neq \mathcal{I}_x \otimes_{\mathcal{O} X, x} k(x) \cong\left(\mathcal{I} \otimes \mathcal{L}^{\otimes n}\right)_x \otimes_{\mathcal{O} X, z} k(x) \quad[\text { since } x \notin Z] \\
& {\left[\text { i.e. } \mathcal{I}_x \nsubseteq \mathfrak{m}_x, \text { since } x \notin Z\right] }
\end{aligned}
$$


Thus $\exists f \in \Gamma\left(X, \mathcal{L}^{\otimes n}\right)$ such that $f(x) \neq 0 \Rightarrow x \in X_f \subset U$.


---




(ii) ⇒ (iii)

Let $U$ be open affine nbhd of $x$ s.t. $\left.\mathcal{L}\right|_U \cong \mathcal{O}_U$.
$\exists f \in \Gamma\left(X, \mathcal{L}^{\otimes n}\right)$ (some $n$ ) s.t. $x \in X_f \subset U$.
Then $X_f=D\left(\varphi\left(\left.f\right|_U\right)\right) \Rightarrow X_f$ is affine.
Thus $\exists$ finite open covering $\left\{X_{f_i}\right\}$ (affine, $f_i \in \Gamma\left(X, \mathcal{L}^{\otimes d_i}\right)$ ).
Note: $X_{f_i^n}=X_{f_i}$ so replacing $f_i$ by powers of $f_i$, can assume all $d_i$ are same.
(iii) ⇒ (i)

Let $\mathcal{F}$ be coherent, $f_i \in \Gamma\left(X, \mathcal{L}^{\otimes d}\right)$ s.t. $\left\{X_{f_i}\right\}$ is open affine cover of $X$.
Then $\left.\mathcal{F}\right|_{X_{f_i}}$ gen by finitely many global secs $g_{i j} \in \Gamma\left(X_{f_i}, \mathcal{F}\right) \ll X_{f_i}$ is affine $\gg$.
Extending sections:
find $N$ s.t. for $m \geq N g_{i j} \otimes f_i^m$ extends to section $\Gamma\left(X, \mathcal{F} \otimes \mathcal{L}^{\otimes d m}\right)$
$\Rightarrow \mathcal{F} \otimes \mathcal{L}^{\otimes d m}$ gen. by global secs
$\Rightarrow \mathcal{L}^{\otimes d}$ ample $\Rightarrow \mathcal{L}$ ample.

Proposition 56: Let $i: Z \hookrightarrow X$ be an immersion between noetherian schemes.
If $\mathcal{L}$ is ample on $X, i^* \mathcal{L}$ is ample on $Z$.
proof.: consider

$$
\begin{gathered}
\Gamma\left(X, \mathcal{L}^{\otimes n}\right) \longrightarrow \Gamma\left(X, i_* i^* \mathcal{L}^{\otimes n}\right) \cong \Gamma\left(Z,\left(i^* \mathcal{L}\right)^{\otimes n}\right) \\
f
\end{gathered}
$$


Since $X_f \cap Z=Z_{f^{\prime}}$, part (ii) of previous proposition implies the result.
Corollary 57: If $\mathcal{L}$ is very ample on $X$ (over $S$ ) then it is ample.
proof.: There is an immersion $X \xrightarrow{i} \mathbb{P}_S^n$ such that $i^* \mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^n}(1) \cong \mathcal{L}$.
But $\mathcal{O}_{\mathrm{P}^n}(1)$ is ample.

Theorem 58: Let $S=\operatorname{Spec}(A)$ (noetherian) and $f: X \rightarrow S$ a finite type morphism, and $\mathcal{L}$ an invertible sheaf on $X$. TFAE
(i) $\mathcal{L}$ is ample
(ii) $\mathcal{L}^{\otimes m}$ is very ample for some $m>0$.
proof.: $(i i) \Rightarrow(i)$ follows from Corollary $57+$ Prop 54.
$\Rightarrow \mathcal{L}^{\otimes m}$ ample $\Rightarrow \mathcal{L}$ ample.
(i) ⇒ (ii)

For some $d \geq 0$, there are finitely many sections $f_i \in \Gamma\left(X, \mathcal{L}^{\otimes d}\right)$ s.t. $X_{f_i}$ affine and $X=\bigcup X_{f_i}$. Write $A_i=\Gamma\left(X_{f_i}, \mathcal{O}_X\right)$ and $\left\{g_{i j}\right\}$ a set of $A$-alg gens of $A_i$.


---


Extending sections $\Rightarrow \exists n_{i j}$ s.t. $a_{i j} \otimes f_i^{n_{i j}}$ extend to sections $g_{i j} \in \Gamma\left(X, \mathcal{L}^{\otimes d n_{i j}}\right)$.
(can assure all $n_{i j}$ are equal $\Rightarrow g_{i j} \in \Gamma\left(X, \mathcal{L}^{\otimes n}\right) \forall i, j$ )
Then $\left\{f_i^n\right\}$ and $\left\{g_{i j}\right\}$ generating sections for $\mathcal{L}^{\otimes n}$
⇒ we obtain a morphism $\phi: X \rightarrow \mathbb{P}_A^N$.
(whole exercise: this is closed immersion (use prop 53).)
Since $\mathcal{L}^{\otimes n} \cong \phi^* \mathcal{O}_{\mathrm{P} N}(1)$, it is very ample. □
