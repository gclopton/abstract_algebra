Theorem 49 [Serre]
Let $X$ be a projective scheme over a noetherian ring $A$. Let $\mathcal{O}_X(1)$ be a very ample invertible sheaf on $X$ . Let $\mathcal{F}$ be a coherent $\mathcal{O}_X$-module. Then $\exists n_0 \in \mathbb{Z}$ s.t. $\forall n \geq n_0, \mathcal{F}(n)$ is generated by finitely many global sections.

Proof: There is a closed immersion $i: X \rightarrow \mathbb{P}_A^n$ such that

$$
\mathcal{O}_X(1) \cong i^* \mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}_A^n}(1)
$$

[ $X$ projective $/ A \Rightarrow$ proper $/ A \Rightarrow$ an immersion $X \rightarrow \mathbb{P}_A^n$ is closed,].
We first reduce to $X=\mathbb{P}_A^n$ as follows:
Since $i$ is finite, $i_* \mathcal{F}$ is coherent.
Since $i_*(\mathcal{F}(n)) \cong\left(i_* \mathcal{F}\right)(n) \ll$ this is the projection formula from HW $\gg$
[i.e. $\left.i_*\left[\mathcal{F} \otimes_{\mathcal{O}_X} i^* \mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^n}(n)\right] \cong i_* \mathcal{F} \otimes_{\mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^n}} \mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^n}(n)\right]$
and $\Gamma(X, \mathcal{F}(n)) \cong \Gamma\left(\mathbb{P}_A^n, i_* \mathcal{F}(n)\right) \Rightarrow$ we can assume $X=\mathbb{P}_A^n$.
<< now our strategy is to use that locally we know the statement and then extend generating sections>>

Now we consider the open affine covering $X=\cup D_{+}\left(x_i\right)$ and choose finitely many sections $t_{i j}^{\prime} \in \Gamma\left(D_{+}\left(x_i\right), \mathcal{F}\right)$ which generate $\mathcal{F}_{D_{+}\left(x_i\right)}$.

By Theorem 42 (extending sections) we can find $n_0$ (independant of $i, j$ ) such that
$x_i^n t_{i j}^{\prime}$ extends to a section $t_{i j} \in \Gamma(X, \mathcal{F}(n))$
$\forall n \geq n_0 \quad \ll x_i \in \Gamma\left(\mathbb{P}_A^n, \mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^n}(1)\right)$ is the section $s$ of theorem $42 \gg$

Theorem 50: Let $k$ be a field, $R$ a f.g. $k$-algebra, $X$ a projective scheme over $R, \mathcal{F}$ a coherent $\mathcal{O}_X-$ module.
Then $\Gamma(X, \mathcal{F})$ is a finitely generated $R$-module.
In particular, when $R=k$ then $\Gamma(X, \mathcal{F})$ is a f.d. vectorspace/ $k$.
