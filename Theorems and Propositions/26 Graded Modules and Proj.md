

Let $\boldsymbol{A}$ be a graded ring.
$M$ : graded module $A$-module.
Define associated q.-coherent module $\widetilde{M}$ on $\operatorname{Proj}(A)$ as follows:
If $f \in A_{+}$is homogeneous, then

$$
\Gamma\left(D_{+}(f), \widetilde{M}\right):=M_{(f)} \tag{*}
$$


$D_+(g)\subset D_+(f)\Rightarrow V_+(f)\supset V_+(g)\Rightarrow g\in \mathrm{rad}((f))_+ \Rightarrow f\in A_g^{\times},$ This means there is an induced $A_{f}$-module map $M_{f}$, hence an $A_{f}$-module map


$$
M_{(f)} \longrightarrow M_{(g)} \quad \text { i.e. } \quad \Gamma\left(D_{+}(f), \widetilde{M}\right) \longrightarrow \Gamma\left(D_{+}(g), \widetilde{M}\right) \tag{**}
$$


There is a unique sheaf on $\operatorname{Proj}(A)$ satisfying properties $(*)$ and $(* *)$.
We obtain a functor [ $X=\operatorname{Proj}(A)$ ]

$$
(-): \operatorname{GrMod}_A \rightarrow \mathrm{QCoh}_{O_x} .
$$


If $A$ is noetherian,

$$
(\sim): \operatorname{GrMod}_A^{\text {fg. }} \rightarrow \text { Coh }_{O_x} .
$$


We have: ( $\sim$ ) is exact, commutes with sums, filtered colims, tensor products; but it is not faithful.

Serre's twisting sheaf:
Given a graded $A$-module $M$, write $M(n), n \in \mathbb{Z}$, for the shifted module $M(n)_d:=M_{n+d}$.
Define

$$
\mathcal{O}_X(n):=\widetilde{A}(n) .
$$


Proposition 44. Assume $A_{+}$is generated by $A_1$. Set $X=\operatorname{Proj}(A)$. Then for any $n \in \mathbb{Z}, \mathcal{O}_X(n)$ is an invertible $\mathcal{O}_X$-mod.

Proof. Need to show $\mathcal{O}_X(n)$ locally free of rank 1 . Note: since $A_{+}$is gen by $A_1$, we have $X= \bigcup_{f \in A_1} D_{+}(f)$.
For any $f \in A_1$,

$$
A_{(f)}=\left(A_f\right)_0 \xrightarrow{f^{-n}}\left(A_f\right)_n=A(n)_0=(A(n))_{(f)}
$$

is an iso. Hence

$$
\left.\left.\mathcal{O}_X(n)\right|_{D_{+}(f)} \cong \mathcal{O}_X\right|_{D_{+}(f)} \quad \text { for any } f \in A_1 \text {. }
$$



Example. Suppose $A$ is a domain. Consider $R=A\left[x_0, \ldots, x_r\right]$ and $X=\operatorname{Proj}(R)=\mathbb{P}_A^r$. Let $f \neq 0 \in R_n, n>0$, and $Z=V_{+}(f) \subset X$.

We have an exact sequence of $\boldsymbol{A}$-modules:

$$
0 \longrightarrow A(-n) \xrightarrow{\cdot f} A \longrightarrow A /(f) \longrightarrow 0 \quad \text { (injective since } A \text { is domain). }
$$

$\Rightarrow$ exact sequence of q.-coh $\mathcal{O}_X$-modules,

$$
0 \longrightarrow \mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}_A^{\prime}}(-n) \longrightarrow \mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}_A^{\prime}} \longrightarrow i_* \mathcal{O}_Z \longrightarrow 0,
$$

where $i: Z \subset \mathbb{P}_A^r$.
Remark. If $A_{+}$is generated by finitely many elts $a_0, \ldots, a_n \in A$, there is a surjective map

$$
A_0\left[T_0, T_1, \ldots, T_n\right] \longrightarrow A, \quad T_i \longmapsto a_i
$$

map of graded $A_0$-algebras.
$\Rightarrow \operatorname{Proj}(A)$ closed subscheme of $\mathbb{P}_{A_0}^n$.
In particular: $X=\operatorname{Proj}(A)$ is qcqs.


Lemma 47. Let $A$ be a graded ring such that $A_{+}$is generated by finitely many elements of $A_1$. Let $\mathcal{F}$ be a quasi-coherent $\mathcal{O}_X$-module and $f \in A_d$ homogeneous. Then

$$
\sigma_f: \Gamma\left(D_{+}(f), \mathcal{F}\right) \xrightarrow{\cong} \Gamma_*(\mathcal{F})_{(f)}
$$

is an iso.
proof. Write $s:=\beta_d(f)$ (where $\left.f \in A_d\right) \in \Gamma\left(X, \mathcal{O}_X(d)\right)$.
Note: $X_s:=\{x \in X \mid s(x) \neq 0\}=D_{+}(f)$, with

$$
s(x)=\bar{s}_x \neq 0 \text { in } \mathcal{O}_X(d)_x \otimes_{\mathcal{O}_{x, x}} k(x) .
$$


Indeed, this can be checked locally:

$$
X_s \cap D_{+}(g)=\left\{x \in \operatorname{Spec}\left(A_{(g)}\right) \mid f / g^d(x) \neq 0\right\}=D_{+}(f) \cap D_{+}(g)
$$


Now by Theorem 42, given any section $t^{\prime} \in \Gamma\left(D_{+}(f), \mathcal{F}\right)$, it can be extended to a section $t \in \Gamma(X, \mathcal{F}(n d))$ for some $n>0$. Define

$$
\sigma_f\left(t^{\prime}\right)=t / s^n \in \Gamma_*(\mathcal{F})_{(s)} \cong \Gamma_*(\mathcal{F})_{(f)} .
$$


The map $\sigma_f: \Gamma\left(D_{+}(f), \mathcal{F}\right) \rightarrow \Gamma_*(\mathcal{F})_{(f)}$ is injective by Theorem 42 , surjective $v$.


Now, given $A$ graded (with $A_{+}$gen by $A_1$ ) and $M$ a graded module, define

$$
\Phi: M \longrightarrow \Gamma_*(\bar{M})
$$

as follows: cover $X=\operatorname{Proj}(A)$ by $\left\{D_{+}(f)\right\}_{f \in A_1}$. If $x \in M_n$, define

$$
\Phi_n^f(x)=\frac{x}{1} \in\left(M_{(f)}\right)_n=\Gamma\left(D_{+}(f), \widetilde{M}(n)\right) .
$$


These glue to yield $\Phi_n(x) \in \Gamma(X, \widetilde{M}(n))$. Hence an $A$-module homomorphism

$$
M=\bigoplus_n M_n \longrightarrow \bigoplus_n \Gamma(X, \widetilde{M}(n))=\Gamma_*(\widetilde{M})
$$


Define $\chi: \Gamma_*(\mathcal{F}) \longrightarrow \mathcal{F}$ as follows. Again cover $X: X=\bigcup_{f \in A_1} D_{+}(f)$. Write $M=\Gamma_*(\mathcal{F})$.

$$
\chi^f: \Gamma\left(D_{+}(f), \widetilde{M}\right)=M_{(f)} \xrightarrow{\cong} \Gamma\left(D_{+}(f), \mathcal{F}\right) .
$$


These glue to yield $\chi$. These are functorial.
