
For nonzero integers $a, b \in \mathbb{Z}-\{0\}$, there is a unique positive integer $l$, called the least common multiple of $a$ and $b$ (or l.c.m. of $a$ and $b$ ), satisfying:
	(a) $a \mid l$ and $b \mid l$ (so $l$ is a common multiple of $a$ and $b$ ), and
	(b) if $a \mid m$ and $b \mid m$, then $l \mid m$ (so $l$ is the least such multiple)


**Note:** Equivalently, for nonzero $a, b$, $|a b|=(a, b) \cdot \operatorname{lcm}(a, b)$
