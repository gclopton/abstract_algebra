
The Division Algorithm: if $a, b \in \mathbb{Z}-\{0\}$, then there exist unique $q, r \in \mathbb{Z}$ such that

$$
a=q b+r \quad \text { and } \quad 0 \leq r<|b|,
$$

When $b>0$, one can take $q=\lfloor a / b\rfloor$ and $r=a-q b$. (Uniqueness refers to the pair $(q, r)$ subject to the stated bounds on $r$.)
